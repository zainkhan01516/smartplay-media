CreatorTech Guide — AdSense Starter Website

Before publishing:
1. Replace YOUR-EMAIL@example.com everywhere with your email.
2. Replace YOURDOMAIN.com in robots.txt and sitemap.xml with your real domain.
3. Add your own logo/brand name if desired.
4. Publish more original, useful articles based on your own experience.
5. Test every link on mobile and desktop.
6. Add cookie/consent tooling if required for your visitors and location.
7. Only add AdSense code after your AdSense account/site setup tells you to.

Important: No website template can guarantee AdSense approval. Google reviews content quality, originality, navigation, policy compliance, and other factors.

A Beginner’s AI Video Workflow for Short-Form Content

Updated October 2026 · 4–6 minute read

Creating short-form videos becomes easier when you separate the process into clear stages: idea, script, visuals, voice, edit, and publishing. Start with one simple topic instead of trying to build a complicated story. Write a short script that has a hook in the first two seconds, a clear middle, and one ending line that gives the viewer a reason to keep watching.

For visuals, create or record only what the script needs. Avoid generating random scenes just because they look impressive. Consistency matters more than quantity. Keep the same character, color mood, aspect ratio, and visual style across the clip.

During editing, remove pauses, add captions that are easy to read, and use sound effects only when they support the action. Export in a vertical format for Shorts, Reels, or TikTok, then review the final video on a phone before publishing.

The most important habit is to save your prompts, project files, captions, and successful hooks. Over time, this becomes your own repeatable production system.

Tip: Adapt these steps to your own workflow and audience. Test one change at a time so you can see what actually improves your results.
